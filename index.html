Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about renting with UniNest

Are there viewing fees?

No! Viewing properties with verified UniNest agents is completely free. Please be cautious of anyone asking for upfront viewing fees.

How do viewings work?

Once you find a property you like, click "Schedule Agent Viewing". An authorized UniNest agent will review your requested time slot and accompany your in-person viewing safely.

How are deposits handled?

Most landlords require a deposit equal to one month's rent. Never pay a deposit before viewing the property and signing a lease agreement.

How do I avoid scams?

We verify our agents, but you should always insist on meeting at the property. Never send money before a viewing and always request a valid receipt.
